# Proposed bounded NightOwls image-access pilot

I authorized validation annotations only. The licence is accepted, but **this image pilot is proposed and not authorized**. GPT Sol fixed the private selection before any image/index retrieval, using only native night image IDs and recording metadata, never GT counts or model outputs. The [configuration](../../configs/research/nightowls_image_access_pilot_20261113.json) pins the annotation and private-manifest hashes.

Select ten hash-ranked night frames from each of the five known recording keys and fourteen from the unkeyed bucket: **64 images maximum**. The unknown bucket is not treated as one sequence. Keep the selection fixed even if it contains empty frames or proves inconvenient to retrieve. Decoded/viewed pilot paths are excluded from a later quantitative image pool; no detector outputs, threshold choice or method fitting occur in this development/access pilot.

The requested future transfer cap is **150 MB of HTTP response bodies**, including at most 4 MB of private ZIP-index metadata, with a **30-minute wall limit**. The complete archive is approximately 57.5 GB and is not an allowed fallback. Require valid HTTPS partial-range responses and a stable archive identity, or stop before reading a full body. Preserve original selected PNGs privately; decode them and compare native dimensions to the annotations. Stop at the first transport/identity/CRC/decode failure or cap, retain partial evidence, and do not replace images or enter a repair loop.

This phase uses local CPU and **zero Colab sessions or GPU operations**. It addresses the concrete transport bottleneck that prevented the previous expanded ACDC run. Selective retrieval and throughput are unverified, and completing all64images is not guaranteed within the cap. No new package, generic streaming framework, full-archive download, redistribution or quantitative research result is part of this request. A successful access pilot would support a separate, concrete acquisition/inference proposal; it would not pass G0.
