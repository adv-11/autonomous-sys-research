Do not claim confirmatory five-percentage-point precision or nominal cluster-bootstrap coverage from the current allocation. The sparse groups justify caution, not a mathematical impossibility claim.

Hold out every related reference and adverse view, including cross-condition dependencies. An unsupported fold is reported as infeasible/undefined rather than repaired by moving images. Do not fit a monotone calibrator and call threshold reselection a recall improvement. Every adaptation arm needs a raw-score target-threshold baseline with the same labels and split.

Do not relabel legacy candidates as Detection 2020. While access is unresolved, design an additional exploratory bridge using ACDC normal references as the source, with global outer-component exclusion. Design and simulation may proceed now; ACDC detector runs wait for the dependency audit and a peer-discussed fold specification. No further open-ended download search is commissioned in this milestone.
5. **Candidate policies are experimental factors.** Start development acquisition at score floor `1e-4` and cap `3000`, recording effective model-specific limits. Prespecify floor sensitivities `{1e-4, 1e-3, 1e-2}` and caps `{100, 300, 1000, 3000}`. This does not declare 3000 uncensored or equalize pipelines. Freeze the eventual study policy on approved source development/fit groups before held-out inference. Never choose it using ACDC target recall. Reuse a maximal cache for nested settings only after checking that the native selector semantics permit it; otherwise run the actual affected setting or mark it unsupported.
6. **Geometry: native primary, common clipping sensitivity.** Keep native preprocessing and original-image geometry in the primary system comparison, explicitly allowing outside-image RT-DETR boxes. A separate sensitivity clips every pipeline's final boxes to the image before matching, preserving candidate identity and reporting any collapsed/removed boxes. Do not silently clip before NMS, change matching or repair boxes. A later common-preprocessing arm is distinct from this geometry sensitivity.
7. **Training budgets count successful optimizer updates.** Also log attempted calls, AMP skips, images presented, effective batch, wall time and peak memory. Advance the declared update-based scheduler/warmup and EMA on successful updates, with implementation verification. A run that cannot obtain its requested successful updates within twice as many attempts stops as a numerical-feasibility failure. Later matched-budget studies must also match effective batch and report data exposure; update counts alone do not equalize compute. No full fine-tuning campaign is authorized by this document.
8. **Keep the core panel controlled.** YOLO11s, YOLO26s and RT-DETRv2-S/R18 remain the core systems. Inspect both YOLO26 learned branches in the same checkpoint, reload independently and set the head explicitly. The O2O/O2M comparison changes heads and post-processing together; it cannot isolate NMS causally. Add repeated seeds before adding more architecture families. DEIM remains a later extension.

## Research direction and falsification

The candidate contribution is **candidate-policy-aware threshold transfer**: distinguish condition-associated candidate-ceiling loss from threshold-removable misses, and test whether conclusions survive changes to candidate collection and class-selection order. Investigate a label-free certificate for when an operating threshold lies above a provable truncation boundary. Such a certificate could establish selection completeness relative to a specified finite candidate pool, **not** recall, semantic correctness or robustness to weather. Ties, class filtering, score floors, NMS truncation and time limits must be explicit.

Novelty is a hypothesis. GPT must compare this direction with the closest calibration, threshold-transfer, risk-control and candidate-selection work using primary sources. A simple top-k prefix fact is not by itself a PhD contribution. If closely covered, narrow or reject the claim and identify what empirical or theoretical result would add substance. Negative controls and negative results stay in the report.

**Milestone correction:** the GPT research task reports close coverage of calibration/shift and cross-class candidate-ranking effects by [Kuzucu et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20459) and [Dave et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.01066v2). The coordinator independently verified their identities and abstract-level findings; the task's detailed source ledger is still in progress. Drop standalone novelty claims for cap effects or a prefix certificate. The contribution hypothesis is now the falsifiable mechanism audit of condition-associated ceiling-versus-threshold loss and policy-dependent transfer conclusions, with the certificate only a supporting control. A reported tracking-calibration paper remains partially accessed; neither missing full text nor a blocked publisher page justifies excluding it as prior work.
